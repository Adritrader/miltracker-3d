Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 9, 2026

1. Who We Are

LiveWar3D (livewar3d.com) is a public, free-to-use military situational-awareness platform. We aggregate publicly available data from open sources — including ADS-B transponder feeds, AIS vessel transponders, GDELT news events, and NASA FIRMS satellite fire data — and present it on an interactive 3D globe.

Contact: legal@livewar3d.com

2. Data We Collect

We collect the minimum data required to operate the service:

  • Locally stored preferences — your map filters, basemap selection, and tracked entities are saved in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device.
  • Cookie consent record — when you accept or decline cookies we store that choice locally in localStorage.
  • Anonymous analytics — if you accept analytics cookies, we may collect aggregated, anonymised data such as page views and session duration. No personally identifiable information (PII) is stored.
  • Server logs — our backend may log IP addresses in standard access logs for security and debugging. Logs are retained for a maximum of 30 days.
  • Account data — if you create a Pro account, we store your email address via our authentication provider (Supabase). We do not store payment card details; payments are processed by Stripe.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not require registration for the free version — no name, email, or personal details are needed.
  • We do not sell, share, or trade personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • We do not use cross-site behavioural tracking or build advertising profiles.

6. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

You have the right to:

  • Access any personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw cookie and analytics consent at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage (DevTools → Application → Local Storage → livewar3d.com → Clear All).
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (for EU residents: your national DPA; for California residents: the CPPA).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at legal@livewar3d.com.

7. Data Retention

Browser-stored preferences are retained until you clear them or uninstall the PWA. Server access logs are deleted after 30 days. Pro account email addresses are retained while the account is active and for 30 days following account deletion.

8. Security

All traffic is encrypted via HTTPS. The backend implements CORS restrictions, rate limiting, and input validation following OWASP best practices. We use parameterised queries to prevent SQL injection and apply content security policies to mitigate cross-site scripting.

9. Children's Privacy

LiveWar3D is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the service after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
