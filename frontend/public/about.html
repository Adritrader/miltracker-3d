What Is LiveWar3D?

LiveWar3D is a free, browser-based situational awareness platform that aggregates publicly available open-source intelligence (OSINT) data and visualises it on an interactive 3D globe. No registration is required. No proprietary data is used.

The platform continuously processes real-time feeds from publicly accessible ADS-B transponder networks, AIS vessel transponders, NASA FIRMS satellite fire data, the GDELT news-event database, and curated ACLED conflict records — and presents everything on a single, navigable 3D globe updated every 30 seconds.

LiveWar3D is intended for informational, educational, and journalistic use. It is not affiliated with any government, military organisation, intelligence agency, or defence contractor, and does not use classified, restricted, or proprietary data sources.

Core Features

✈ Live Military Aircraft

Track over 100 categories of military aircraft — fighters, ISR platforms, tankers, transport jets, helicopters, and drones — in real time using public ADS-B transponder data.

⚓ Warship Positions

Monitor active navy vessels including aircraft carriers, destroyers, frigates, and submarines across the world's oceans using public AIS transponder feeds.

🌍 Active Conflict Zones

Colour-coded danger zones mark areas of active armed conflict, based on curated ACLED event data and open geopolitical intelligence sources updated daily.

🔥 NASA FIRMS Fire Data

Real-time satellite fire hotspot data from NASA's Fire Information for Resource Management System (FIRMS), identifying thermal anomalies including potential airstrikes and industrial fires.

📰 Live War News

Breaking conflict and defence news from GDELT — geocoded and pinned to the globe so you can read what is happening at each location at a glance.

📷 Conflict Cameras

Curated live camera feeds from active conflict regions including Ukraine, Taiwan, the Korean DMZ, and the Middle East — embedded directly in the map interface.

🤖 AI Threat Analysis

On-demand AI-generated intelligence summaries for selected aircraft and vessels — including mission assessment, historical context, and estimated threat level.

🔍 Search & Track

Search for any aircraft by callsign or ICAO hex code, or any vessel by name or MMSI. Add them to your personal tracking list and receive movement alerts.

Data Sources

LiveWar3D aggregates exclusively from public, open-source data networks. No proprietary, classified, or government-restricted data is used at any time. All sources are openly accessible to any member of the public.

Data Type Source Update Frequency
Military aircraft positions adsb.lol, adsb.fi (cooperative ADS-B network) Every 30 seconds
Warship / naval vessel positions Public AIS aggregators Every 30 seconds
Conflict and geopolitical news GDELT Project (gdeltproject.org) Every 15 minutes
Satellite fire / thermal hotspots NASA FIRMS (firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov) Every 3 hours
Armed conflict event records ACLED (acleddata.com) Daily
Live conflict camera feeds Public YouTube live streams, EarthCam Real-time (on demand)
Military base locations Publicly available open geospatial datasets Static (periodically updated)

Who Uses LiveWar3D?

LiveWar3D was built for anyone who wants to understand global military activity through publicly available data:

  • Journalists and war correspondents — quickly identify and cross-reference aircraft, vessels, or events in a conflict area they are covering.
  • OSINT researchers and analysts — correlate open-source intelligence from multiple data streams in a single interface.
  • Students and academics — study geopolitics, international security, and military operations using real-world data.
  • Defence and security enthusiasts — follow military developments worldwide with the depth of a professional tool and the accessibility of a consumer app.
  • General public — stay informed about world events with a visual, data-driven perspective that goes beyond headlines.

Accuracy & Disclaimers

Important: All data displayed on LiveWar3D is sourced from public cooperative transponder networks and open databases. Coverage is not global. Aircraft and vessels may operate without broadcasting transponder signals. Data may be delayed, incomplete, or inaccurate.

LiveWar3D makes no warranty as to the accuracy, completeness, or fitness for any purpose of the data displayed. The platform is designed for situational awareness and informational purposes only. It must not be used for navigation, operational planning, or any safety-critical decision-making.

The platform is not affiliated with any military, government agency, or intelligence service. All content is derived from unclassified, publicly available sources.
